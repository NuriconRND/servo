## Traps

* **`composition_grid()` (1734-1761) is the pacing clock's timer fallback.** Six of its seven callers are log lines in `output_grid.rs`, but the seventh — `lib.rs:67` → `servo::dwm_composition_grid` → `winit_wall/main.rs:1235` `snap_to_dwm_grid_at` — is the grid the pacing thread sleeps to when it is not blocking on `WaitForVBlank`. Deleting it stops the wall's timer arm from snapping to the composition grid. Its sibling `dwm_timing()` *is* removable; they look identical and are not.
* **`note_frame_statistics` (2566-2615) / `IDCompositionDevice::GetFrameStatistics` is the animation sample grid, not a statistic.** `note_dcomp_stat` writes `output_grid::COMPOSITION` deliberately *outside* the `COLLECT` gate (output_grid.rs:1373-1377, comment: "격자 갱신은 계측 게이트 밖이다 … B2 가 통째로 무력해진다"). `lead_to_next_composition` → `painter::paint_animation_sample_at` uses it to choose the instant at which the page's animation is sampled. Remove it and `gfx_sample_lead_periods` silently degrades to "sample at now" — a pixel-level regression with no error.
* **`painter.rs:4286` is a two-armed gate.** `!*DCOMP_BIND_PROF && SAMPLE_LEAD_PERIODS.is_none()` — the first arm is instrumentation, the second is the feature. Stripping `DCOMP_BIND_PROF` must preserve the second arm. The in-file comment records that this exact mistake was made once before ("B1 에서 프로브를 진단 플래그 뒤에 두어 똑같이 당한 적이 있다").
* **`probe_partial_present` (779-800)** reads like a diagnostic probe. It calls `GetBuffer(1)` once per content-swap and its boolean selects the partial-Present path (`sc.partial_present`, used at 4801). Load-bearing.
* **`quantize_update_rect` / `UPDATE_QUANTUM_PX` (308-357)** look like a measurement knob (`gfx_dcomp_update_quantum_px`). They change the `BeginDraw` update RECT, i.e. the texture DComp allocates and the pixels committed. Load-bearing — and their seven unit tests sit inside `mod commit_probe_tests`, a module otherwise full of instrumentation tests. Deleting that module wholesale deletes behaviour coverage.
* **`PRESENT_SYNC_INTERVAL` (1428-1439)** emits a `warn!` at init and is named like a profiling knob. It is the SyncInterval argument to `Present` (3112) and decides whether the renderer thread blocks on vblank.
* **`withheld_frames` (740)** is incremented next to a warn and reset next to a `[dcomp-dbg]` line, but it drives `DEMOTE_AFTER_WITHHOLD` (4856) — the swap-chain → virtual demotion. Only the `== WITHHOLD_WARN_FRAMES` warn (4861-4867) is log-only.
* **`present_ns` / `presents` locals in `end_frame` (4706-4712, 4818-4824)** wrap the actual `Present`/`present1_partial` calls and their success booleans. The stopwatch is instrumentation; the call and the `ok` result are not.
* **`needs_flush` / `device.gl().flush()` (4554-4568)** — the `flushes` / `flush_skipped` counters are instrumentation; the `needs_flush` predicate and the flush itself are a correctness fix for the External path.
* **`RemoveAllVisuals()` (5159)** sits inside a `removeall_start` stopwatch. The call and its position (inside the device guard, paired with the `AddVisual` loop) are load-bearing — a past bug flashed the taskbar through an empty visual tree.
* **`self.commit_pending = true;` (5214)** sits immediately above `deferred_commits += 1`. The flag is the deferred-commit self-recovery for shells that never call `flush_deferred_commit` (servoshell); the counter is not.
* **`raw_commit`'s `work` snapshot (2249-2251) is taken *before* `Commit()` on purpose** and sits inside the device guard. Removing it is safe and shrinks the critical section — but the surrounding comments about "guard holds only `Commit()`" are the invariant the file was fixed three times to establish; do not take the comment removal as licence to add anything else there.
* **`commit_guard::remember_device_monitor` (2603) is called from the *kept* `note_frame_statistics`.** Its only consumer is the commit-failure log. If `note_commit_failure` keeps its `out=` field, this call must stay even though it looks like bookkeeping.
